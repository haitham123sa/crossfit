H-FORGE
Built for the bar

Strength tools.
One free app.

H-FORGE helps strength athletes manage barbell loading, calculate percentages, time workouts, and keep track of lifting personal records. Open it in your browser or add it to your phone’s home screen.

Open H-FORGE · Free sign-in

Use a Google account or email and password. A free account is required before using the training tools.

01 · BARBELL

Barbell plate calculator

Plan loading for your target weight and manage available plates in kilograms or pounds.

02 · PERCENTAGES

Weightlifting percentage calculator

Calculate training weights from your chosen lifting max for strength and Olympic lifting sessions.

03 · WORKOUT TIMERS

WOD timer modes

Run AMRAP, EMOM, For Time, and Tabata workouts with on-device timer controls.

04 · PERSONAL RECORDS

Private PR tracker

Record your personal best lifts and save them to your signed-in profile for cloud synchronization.

Train on your phone

H-FORGE is a progressive web app (PWA), so you can open it from your browser and add a shortcut to your mobile home screen without downloading an app-store package. Some features and previously loaded files may be available offline; cloud account updates need connectivity.

Private training data

Your records are associated with your Firebase account and are not publicly listed on this website. You can export a backup from Settings, review the privacy policy, or read the account deletion instructions.
